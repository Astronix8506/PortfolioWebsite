Spread your Wings

2024-2024

Status: Paused

Spread your Wings is a roguelike top-down scroller in which you play as a bird spreading the communist regime to your fellow avian friends, gaining influence, battling intrusive thoughts, and acquiring powers to stop the Americans from shooting you down. This was a submission for the Pirate Software - Game Jam 14.

CodeQuax

2024-2024

Status: Paused

CodeQuax is a developer platform designed for networking, job searching, and participating in events like game jams and hackathons. This project was created as part of the DACC lean six sigma green belt certification, aiming to provide a central hub for developers to connect and collaborate.

Features

  • Networking: Connect with other developers, share ideas, and collaborate on projects.
  • Job Listings: Browse job opportunities tailored for developers in various fields.
  • Events: Join game jams, hackathons, and other tech events to showcase skills and learn from peers.
